Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

Chand Kabadi Wala ("we", "our", "us") operates the Chand Kabadi Wala mobile application and website. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We collect your phone number for authentication via OTP. When you register as a vendor, we may collect documents like Aadhar, PAN, and shop photos for verification.

Location Data: To facilitate scrap pickups, we collect your location data (GPS coordinates) to show nearby vendors or to track the route for a pickup.

Camera/Photos: We require access to your camera and gallery to upload identification documents and proof of scrap collection.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide our services, verify identities, contact you for pickups, and process payments/donations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your contact information is shared between the customer and the assigned vendor only to complete a pickup request.

4. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and secure database connections to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@chandkabadiwala.com
